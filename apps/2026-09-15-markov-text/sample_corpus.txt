The old lighthouse stood at the edge of the cliff. It watched the waves roll in every morning. The keeper climbed the spiral stairs before dawn. He polished the great lens until it gleamed. Ships far at sea relied on its steady beam. On foggy nights the horn called out across the water. Gulls circled the tower and cried into the wind. The keeper kept a quiet log of every storm. Each sunrise painted the horizon in soft gold. He believed the light kept the whole coast safe.
